SLIDE 1: METHODS  (no new image; keep your icons)
-------------------------------------------------
Toolchain: k6 load testing tool with the FastAPI Bloom's service
(DistilBERT) and Supabase Free tier.

Hardware: AI service on a laptop (Ryzen 7 5700U, 8 cores, 16 GB RAM).

Workloads Tested: single-batch baselines, concurrency scaling
(1–64 instructors analyzing at once), 100 simultaneous analyses,
re-analysis with cache, and exam day (1,000 students + 100 instructors).

Big number on the right:  1–64 instructors at once


SLIDE 2: RESULTS: STABILITY  (image: 2-stability-chart.png)
------------------------------------------------------------
Stability: 100% success under peak load: all 100 simultaneous
50-question analyses finished (before the fixes, 20 timed out).
A fair queue and a result cache cut service memory from 5.5 GB to 1.4 GB.


SLIDE 3: RESULTS: RESPONSE TIME  (image: 3-response-time-chart.png)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Response Time: about 2.4 s per 50-question batch. Re-analysis after
editing improved from 42.5 s to 5.5 s with 20 instructors working at once.


SLIDE 4: RESULTS: CONCURRENCY LIMIT  (image: 4-concurrency-chart.png)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Concurrency Limit: CPU-bound ceiling of 40–47 batches per minute.
Up to 4 simultaneous analyses meet the 15 s target (p95 10.6 s);
beyond that, requests wait in a fair queue instead of failing.


SLIDE 5: RESULTS: EXAM DAY  (image: 5-exam-day-chart.png)  ← NEW SLIDE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Exam Day: the AI classified all 325 batches (p95 3.6 s at a realistic
pace). The Supabase Free-tier database (0.5 GB RAM) ran out of memory
after 6–11 minutes; 1,000 students alone passed with 0 errors.
A real exam day needs a larger database plan.


SLIDE 6: CONCLUSION  (optional; no image needed)
------------------------------------------------
• Stable: 100% of batches classified in every test after the fixes.
• Fast: 2.4 s per 50-question batch; 5.5 s re-analysis.
• Limit: 40–47 batches/minute on a laptop CPU; up to 4 at once
  within 15 s.
• System-wide: the Free-tier database, not the AI, limits exam day.
• Recommendation: deploy the AI on a server with more CPU cores and
  use a larger database plan for real exam days.
